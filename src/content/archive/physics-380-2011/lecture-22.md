We are continuing our short introduction to dynamical systems.

## Main lecture

1.  Once systems become more than 1d, other arrangements but fixed points are possible. One may have oscillations as well. Consider a system of two interacting genes: $\begin{array}{l}\frac{dX}{dt}=C_X+\frac{V_X}{1+(Y/K_Y)^2}-rX \\ \frac{dY}{dt}=C_Y +\frac{V_Y X^2}{1+(X/K_X)^2}-rY\end{array}$.
    - The point of zero derivatives are: for X: $X=\left(C_X+\frac{V_X}{1+(Y/K_Y)^2}\right)/r$, and for Y, $Y=\left(C_Y +\frac{V_Y X^2}{1+(X/K_X)^2}\right)/r$.
