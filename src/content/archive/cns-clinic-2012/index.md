The Clinic sessions, lead by Emory and Georgia Tech faculty, explore modern concepts and techniques for data acquisition, analysis, and interpretation in computational neuroscience. The Clinic is intended for researchers, from undergraduate students to faculty, working or training to work in the field.

**When:** Every second Thursday, 5:30 pm (refreshments provided)

**Where:** 1462 Clifton Rd, Room DS109
